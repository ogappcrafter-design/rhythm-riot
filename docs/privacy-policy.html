Privacy Policy — Rhythm Riot

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Rhythm Riot ("the app", "we") is a rhythm game published by the developer of Rhythm Riot. This policy explains how the app handles information. In short: the app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. Rhythm Riot does not collect, record, or transmit any personal or sensitive user data. We have no servers and no analytics.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your game progress — best scores, grades, unlocked difficulties, statistics and settings — locally on your device only, using standard on-device storage. This data never leaves your device, is never sent to us or any third party, and is removed if you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Permissions

The app requests the INTERNET permission that its framework includes by default. Rhythm Riot does not use it to send or receive any personal data; all gameplay content is bundled in the app and runs offline.

Third-party services, ads and purchases

Rhythm Riot contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
